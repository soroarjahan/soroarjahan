Who is Soroar Jahan?

Soroar Jahan serves as a bridge between abstract theory and real-world impact. Armed with deep expertise in logical thinking and AI, he moved beyond the classroom to co-found purpose-driven startups alongside his Engineering degree. He addresses food security through FarmFilo, transforms learning through STEM Wander, and enhances digital efficiency through BitOriole. His leadership is evident in his role as Campus Director of the Hult Prize, often called the "Nobel Prize for students". Soroar creates value where others see obstacles, crafting a legacy that blends the precision of a Programmer with the heart of a social Entrepreneur.
